# Video openings, hooks and audience retention

**Type:** research-backed content experimentation, not an installable skill.

Official sources:
- [YouTube Analytics — audience retention and key moments](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415)
- [YouTube Analytics — understand your audience](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314416)

Plan multiple openings for the same audience need, then test them using comparable distribution conditions and measured early retention, average watch duration, reach and downstream actions. Track topic, promise, opening frame, spoken hook, delivery and audience segment. Distinguish an engaging hook from misleading clickbait: the content should deliver the opening promise.

Do **not** treat an influencer's “10x views” claim, “100 proven hooks” giveaway, or a fixed scoring formula as empirical evidence. The YouTube documentation describes measurement capabilities, not universal Instagram ranking factors. Coordinate with `docs/product-experience/analytics-experimentation-growth.md` and avoid duplicating general measurement guidance.
