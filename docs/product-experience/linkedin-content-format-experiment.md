# LinkedIn content format hypothesis from a single creator experiment

**Discovery:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/Df3YLvFCyDA/ — transcribed spoken content, creator's self-report, not independently audited.

## Observed claim

The creator reported **62 posts over 50 days**, approximately **244,832 impressions**, with roughly **85% of impressions concentrated in one story post**. Of categorized content: 22 educational, 26 personal-story, six "give them their flowers" acknowledgment posts; the named categories account for 54 of 62 posts, leaving eight unclassified in the spoken summary.

The creator said plain educational posts typically underperformed, while personal stories and recognition posts had stronger reach in this experiment. This is a single observational series with extreme outlier concentration, unknown follower-growth baseline, algorithmic variation and no randomized comparison.

## Reusable method

- Define a business goal beyond impressions (qualified visits, leads, trials and purchases).
- Pre-register formats: direct educational content, first-person story with lesson, appreciation/recognition, and mixed formats. Record impressions, engagement, profile visits, click-throughs, first-party signups, week, time, audience size, and distribution channel.
- Report **median, trimmed mean, distribution, P90/P95 and outlier share** by format. A single viral post can swamp totals and create survivorship bias.
- Randomize posting times or counterbalance content types over weeks where feasible; never publish invented testimonials or faux praise.
- Compare cohorts after sufficient observations; avoid claiming that stories *cause* more impressions based on one account.
- Adopt a stop/continue/iterate rule based on downstream conversion, not vanity metrics.

For valuable content principles, see Google Search Central's people-first guidelines: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content . This source supports audience-first content but **does not validate LinkedIn-specific algorithm performance**.
