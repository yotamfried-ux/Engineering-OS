# App Store Optimization (ASO) — official platform documentation

Primary sources: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/create-product-page-optimization-tests/overview-of-product-page-optimization ; https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/ ; https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6227309

Plan truthful title/keywords, concise value proposition, screenshots that accurately demonstrate key tasks, previews, localized metadata and review-response workflow. Apple's Product Page Optimization permits testing alternate icons, screenshots and previews and measuring conversion; requirements and eligibility depend on store and app state. Google Play store listing experiments have their own controls. Never assume “first three screenshots” are universally displayed in all placements or that a fixed 14-item checklist proves conversion. Measure install conversion, quality and downstream retention. Coordinate with existing release preparation guides.
