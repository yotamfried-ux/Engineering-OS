## Beyond UI: skills agents should apply

1. **Product discovery:** user segments, jobs-to-be-done, problem interviews, competitor analysis, assumptions log, value proposition.
2. **Information architecture:** content hierarchy, navigation, search, wayfinding and findability.
3. **UX writing and content strategy:** action-first microcopy, helpful errors, onboarding, trust and localized Hebrew copy.
4. **Behavioral science:** cognitive load, recognition vs recall, choice architecture, defaults, feedback and commitment. Use to reduce friction, not manipulate.
5. **CRO:** map funnel steps, inspect friction, propose hypotheses, run experiments, review effect and guardrails.
6. **Pricing and packaging:** plan clarity, transparent pricing, fair trial/cancellation and clear upgrade paths.
7. **Trust & credibility:** transparent claims, privacy explanations, real testimonials, clear support and no fabricated urgency.
8. **Lifecycle UX:** onboarding, activation, notifications, re-engagement, retention and respectful offboarding.
9. **Localization:** Hebrew-first RTL layouts, numerals, dates, mixed LTR content, accessible copy and Israeli user expectations.
10. **Research operations:** consent, recruiting, task scripts, observation notes, evidence strength and ethical handling of recordings.

## CRO process

Define baseline -> segment funnel -> identify largest friction -> generate hypotheses -> prioritize by impact/confidence/effort -> prototype -> usability test or randomized experiment -> inspect confidence intervals and guardrails -> document decision.

**Do not assume** that red CTAs, scarcity messages or extra motion inherently increase conversion. Measure in the actual product and audience. Distinguish correlation from causation. Avoid deceptive design patterns, fake social proof, hidden subscriptions and prechecked consent.

## Agent deliverable template

- Problem / user segment / job to be done
- Current flow and baseline evidence
- Proposed design with rationale and research links
- Hypothesis and expected direction of impact
- Accessibility / privacy / performance risks
- Metrics and test plan
- Result and decision, including null or negative findings
