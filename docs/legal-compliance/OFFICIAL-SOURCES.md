## Documentation provenance contract

For each legal statement store `source_url`, `publisher`, `territory`, `version/date`, `applicability hypothesis`, `legal vs platform vs best-practice`, `last_verified` and reviewer. Cite current statutes and regulators above blog posts; never assert a standard as a legal duty everywhere. If an official source moved or contradicts this atlas, mark `STALE / REQUIRE COUNSEL`, not PASS.

### Especially important legal traps

- Israel's post-Amendment-13 registration system **is not** an automatic registration duty for every user database.
- Israel's PPA serious-incident reporting rules are **not identical** to the GDPR breach rule.
- UK cookies guidance has been updated; prefer the current technology guidance to old cookie articles.
- The US FTC's 2024 Click-to-Cancel announcement **does not by itself describe the 2026 legal state**.
- A rights request may require secure identity checks, limited statutory retention, processor coordination and jurisdiction-specific exceptions.
- Data-residency and cross-border-transfer rules may differ from where a company is incorporated.
