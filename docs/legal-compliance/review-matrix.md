# Legal applicability review matrix — Israel-first

For every app, record YES / NO / UNKNOWN, reasoning, source, counsel review and date. UNKNOWN is not automatically NOT APPLICABLE.

| Trigger | Review workstream | Official starting point |
|---|---|---|
| Any collection of identifiable personal data | Israeli Privacy Protection Law (incl. Amendment 13), security regulations, notices, retention, processors, breach response | https://www.gov.il/he/departments/the_privacy_protection_authority |
| Website/app selling to Israeli consumers | Consumer Protection Law: distance sales, cancellation, subscription/price disclosures | https://www.gov.il/he/departments/consumer_protection_and_fair_trade_authority |
| Israeli public-facing digital service | Accessibility applicability, Israeli Standard 5568, exceptions and declarations | https://www.gov.il/he/departments/commission_for_equal_rights_of_persons_with_disabilities |
| Promotional SMS/email | Section 30A of Communications Law, consent/opt-out and recordkeeping | https://www.gov.il/he/departments/ministry_of_communications |
| UGC/media uploads | Copyright licensing, reporting/takedown, moderation, repeat violations, platform rules | https://www.gov.il/he/departments/ministry_of_justice |
| AI-generated claims or AI interacting with users | Truthful advertising, transparent user experience, sensitive decisions and applicable sector rules | https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing |
| Apple or Android app distribution | Privacy disclosures, account deletion, UGC safety, SDK declarations, permissions, store policies | https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/ |
| EU targeting or EU personal data processing within GDPR territorial scope | GDPR lawful basis, notices, data subject rights, transfers and DPA obligations | https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj |
| US-directed user uploads and US safe-harbor strategy | Assess 17 USC 512 DMCA designated agent and notices; not universal | https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/ |
| Payments | Payment provider obligations, PCI scope, Israeli tax/accounting and consumer requirements | https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/ |

## Guardrails
Never paste a boilerplate privacy policy or arbitration waiver without verifying actual app behavior and enforceability. Verify the official current law, regulations and guidance at review time. Flag counsel-required and jurisdiction-uncertain cases explicitly.
