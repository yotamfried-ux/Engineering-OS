## Product

Research comparable experiences and player motivations. Define a compelling first minute, core loop, progression, balanced rewards, social reasons to return, and sustainable content production. Test with target-device players; distinguish fun from short-lived novelty.

## Monetization options (eligibility and terms change)

Game passes (durable entitlements), developer products (repeatable purchases), subscriptions, paid private servers, seasonal progression products, immersive ads, creator rewards, avatar/creator-store commerce and brand collaborations where eligible. Check current eligibility, region, age, disclosures and payout terms before recommending or implementing. Never assume all channels are enabled for an account.

Design for player trust: clear prices and value, no misleading urgency, no unauthorized purchases, no gambling-like paid randomness without applicable disclosures and policy checks. Validate purchase receipts server-side, grant idempotently, log failures and support recovery.

## Growth and measurement

Instrument discovery impressions → joins → tutorial completion → first fun moment → D1/D7/D30 retention → social invitations → payer conversion. Monitor session quality, funnel abandonment, cohort behavior, ARPDAU, ARPPU, LTV and campaign costs. Avoid treating impressions or gross Robux as cash profit. Account for platform fees, DevEx eligibility, operating costs and attribution uncertainty.

Optimize thumbnails, titles, metadata, onboarding and retention before scaling advertising. Run controlled experiments with guardrail metrics, representative samples and documented hypotheses. Build LiveOps cadence around meaningful updates rather than manipulative streaks.

## Governance

Review Roblox terms, community standards, content maturity, IP ownership, UGC/moderation, privacy, child safety, regional consumer law and ads policies. For Israel-based operators, also route to existing `docs/legal-compliance/`; no document substitutes for legal advice.
